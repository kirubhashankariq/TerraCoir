Fiber Science

Choosing the Right Botanical Fiber For Your Project

Understand the mechanical differences between Coir Bristle, Decorticated Golden Jute, and Natural Flax blends.

High Stiffness

Coconut Husk Coir

Extracted from outer coconut shells. Extremely high lignin content makes it water-resistant, rot-resistant, and rigid. Ideal for door scraping and outdoor civil erosion nets.

Soft & Lustrous

Golden Bengal Jute

Harvested from bast stem fibers. Highly pliable with a soft underfoot feel and a natural golden sheen. Best suited for interior rugs, market bags, and breathable agricultural sacks.

Hybrid Blend

Coir & Jute Blends

Combines the structural rigidity of a coir fiber core with the smooth, tactile exterior of spun jute cord. Used in heavy-duty nesting baskets and hallway runners.

Care Protocols

Care & Maintenance for Botanical Fiber Goods

Simple steps to maintain the beauty and durability of your natural coir mats and jute rugs for years.

Simply lift the mat and shake it vigorously outside to dislodge collected dirt. You can also vacuum using the suction-only brush attachment. Avoid soaking in standing water to preserve the natural latex backing.
Blot immediately with a clean dry towel. Do not rub, as friction may push the liquid deeper into the plant fibers. For light stains, dab gently with a cloth lightly dampened with cold water and mild dish soap, then dry thoroughly with a fan.
Some loose bristle fiber shedding is completely normal during the first 2-3 weeks of use. This is loose natural fiber trimming and will naturally subside after regular sweeping or light vacuuming.
