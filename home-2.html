Engineering Rigor

ASTM Compliant Testing in Dedicated Mill Laboratories

Every batch of coir geotextiles and industrial burlap is subjected to standardized tensile, puncture, and water permittivity testing before release.

ASTM D4595

Wide-Width Tensile Test

Measures ultimate tensile strength and elongation in both machine and cross-machine directions under continuous strain.

ASTM D4491

Water Permittivity & Flow

Evaluates the volumetric flow rate of water through geotextile aperture meshes under constant and falling head conditions.

ASTM D4355

Solar UV Weathering

Accelerated xenon-arc weathering tests to confirm fiber mechanical integrity over the critical 36-month revegetation cycle.
