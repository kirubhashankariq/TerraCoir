The TerraCoir Manifesto

Rooted in Soil. Woven by Artisans. Engineered for Tomorrow.

Founded in 2011, TerraCoir was built on a singular conviction: humanity does not need synthetic petroleum plastics to furnish functional living spaces or stabilize civil landscapes. The Earth has already provided the ultimate high-tensile fibers in coconut husks and golden jute.

Geographic Roots

Two Distinct Coastal Hubs of Craft & Industry

We operate twin production hubs positioned directly at the natural agricultural epicenters of coconut coir and golden jute.

Cochin, Kerala South Indian Coast

The Coconut Coir Capital

Situated alongside the historic saline backwaters of Alleppey and Cochin, our southern facility specializes in traditional brackish water retting, mechanical bristle decortication, high-density tufted entrance mats, and ASTM civil geotextile weaving.

  • ✓ 450 artisanal pit looms and 12 automatic jacquard heads
  • ✓ Dedicated solar drying yards handling 60 metric tons weekly
  • ✓ Direct deepwater port access at Cochin Container Terminal
Kolkata, West Bengal Ganges Delta Belt

The Golden Jute Delta

Located on the fertile alluvial plains of the Hooghly River, our eastern mill processes ultra-fine, long-staple Corchorus olitorius jute into lustrous interior area rugs, braided baskets, fine market tote bags, and hydrocarbon-free agricultural burlap sacks.

  • ✓ Vegetable oil treatment (VOT) certified spinning machinery
  • ✓ Zero mineral oil contamination guarantee for food coffee bags
  • ✓ International export loading at Kolkata Port docks
Master Craftsmanship

From Coconut Grove to Finished Masterpiece

Discover the exact five-stage transformation honoring generational Kerala and Bengal weaving traditions.

01 • Retting

Natural Soaking

Husks and stalks are submerged in natural tidal lagoons where microorganisms soften the woody.

02 • Combing

Fiber Carding

Rotating steel wire drums comb out the golden bristle fibers, sorting them by tensile strength and length.

03 • Spinning

Yarn Twisting

Women artisans spin the carded fibers into uniform 2-ply and 3-ply yarns using traditional spinning.

04 • Looms

Hand Weaving

Artisans operate heavy wooden pit looms, interlocking the warp and weft into dense architectural weaves.

05 • Finishing

Shearing & QC

Hand-sheared to uniform pile heights, bonded with natural tree latex, and inspected against ASTM.
