The Circular Journey of Pure Botanical Fibers

Every thread traces directly to renewable coconut husks and seasonal jute crops, transforming agricultural byproducts into architectural utility.

01

Backwater Husk Retting

Harvested coconut husks soak in natural saline estuaries in Kerala for 6-10 months, naturally softening the lignified bristle fibers without chemicals.

02

Golden Jute Carding

Long-staple Corchorus jute stalks from the Bengal riverbanks are mechanically decorticated and carded into smooth, lustrous golden yarn.

03

Zero-Effluent Weaving

Master artisans use traditional wooden pit looms and solar-powered jacquard heads with plant-derived non-toxic mineral dyes.

04

100% Soil Composting

At end-of-life, our coir and jute decompose completely into nutrient-rich humus within 24 months, leaving zero synthetic microplastics behind.
